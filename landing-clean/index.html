Safe chat

Every message is screened
before anyone sees it

AI moderation screens every message in every thread — group chat, small groups, parent chat. Flagged messages are held and routed to the right adult.

  • Held, not deleted. Flagged messages wait for human review, so nothing disappears without an adult seeing it.
  • No member-to-member DMs. Students talk in group threads and 1:1 with their pastor or leader — nowhere else.
  • Parents get their own thread. Created automatically with you and your leaders when their teen joins.
  • Nobody is exempt. Leaders and pastors go through the same screening as every student.

The game

A garden that only grows when they read

Finishing a day's plan earns water and XP. Water grows each student's backyard, plant by plant, over weeks of showing up — and it fades when they stop, which brings students back better than any push notification.

Water and XPEarned only by reading. Streaks multiply both, so steady beats bingeing.
Plants track plansEach plant grows a stage with every day of a plan. A finished book is a plant in full bloom.
Wilting is reversibleMiss a stretch and the garden fades. Come back and it recovers.
